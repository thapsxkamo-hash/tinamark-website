Industries We Serve

Tinamark provides tailored procurement and supply solutions across a wide range of industries.

Mining & Industrial

Tinamark supports mining and industrial operations with reliable supply of PPE, safety equipment, and industrial consumables. We understand the high risk nature of mining environments and provide products that meet rigorous safety standards. Our responsive supply chain ensures that your site never faces downtime due to lack of essential supplies. From head to toe protection to specialized mining tools, we deliver quality assured products that keep your workforce safe and your operations running efficiently.

Construction

For construction sites across South Africa, Tinamark supplies essential PPE, safety gear, and site consumables. We know that construction projects run on tight schedules, and any delay in supplies can cost time and money. Our fast turnaround times and bulk supply capabilities ensure that your site remains fully stocked with certified safety equipment, from hard hats and high vis vests to fall protection gear. We work with leading manufacturers to deliver products that comply with all relevant safety standards, giving you peace of mind while your team builds.

Engineering

Engineering workshops and manufacturing facilities require precision supplies and reliable protective equipment. Tinamark partners with engineering firms to provide everything from workshop consumables to specialized safety gear. We understand the technical requirements of engineering environments and source products that meet exact specifications. Whether you need maintenance supplies, workshop tools, or PPE for your engineering team, we deliver quality products that support precision work and workplace safety.

Medical & Healthcare

Healthcare facilities require sterile, high quality medical supplies delivered with urgency and precision. Tinamark supplies hospitals, clinics, and medical practices with surgical consumables, disposable products, and medical grade PPE. We source from trusted manufacturers who meet strict quality standards, ensuring that your medical team has access to reliable products when they need them. From surgical masks and gloves to feeding tubes and catheters, we support the healthcare sector with dependable supply solutions.

Abattoirs & Food Processing

Food processing facilities and abattoirs have unique hygiene and safety requirements. Tinamark supplies specialized PPE and cleaning products designed for food safe environments. We understand the importance of maintaining strict hygiene standards and provide products that help your facility comply with health regulations. From waterproof aprons and cut resistant gloves to sanitization supplies, we deliver quality products that protect your workers and maintain food safety standards.

Security Companies

Security personnel need durable, professional grade uniforms and protective equipment. Tinamark supplies security companies with high quality uniforms, tactical gear, and safety accessories that enhance both comfort and protection. We understand the demands of round the clock security operations and provide products that withstand daily wear while maintaining a professional appearance. From reflective vests to duty boots, we help keep your security team equipped and visible.

Cleaning Companies

Professional cleaning companies require reliable supplies to maintain hygiene standards across their client sites. Tinamark provides cleaning and janitorial supplies, including PPE for cleaning staff, disposable products, and industrial cleaning solutions. We support cleaning contractors with bulk ordering and recurring supply arrangements, ensuring that you never run out of essential products. From gloves and masks to cleaning chemicals and disposable wipes, we help you deliver exceptional service to your clients.

Warehousing & Logistics

Warehouses and logistics hubs require safety equipment that protects workers in fast paced environments. Tinamark supplies warehouse operators with PPE, safety gear, and operational supplies that support efficiency and workplace safety. From high visibility clothing to safety footwear, we provide products that meet the demands of loading bays, storage facilities, and distribution centers. Our bulk supply options and fast delivery ensure that your warehouse stays compliant with safety regulations.

Schools & Institutions

Educational institutions and public facilities need reliable supplies for daily operations and emergency preparedness. Tinamark provides schools with essential products including cleaning supplies, hygiene products, and basic first aid equipment. We understand the importance of maintaining safe, healthy environments for students and staff, and we deliver quality products that help institutions meet their operational needs. From hand sanitizers to disposable masks, we support schools in keeping their communities safe.

Agriculture & Farming

Agricultural operations require durable, practical protective equipment that can withstand outdoor conditions. Tinamark supplies farms and agribusinesses with PPE, workwear, and safety gear designed for agricultural environments. From sun protection and waterproof clothing to chemical resistant gloves for pesticide application, we provide products that keep farm workers safe and comfortable. Our responsive service ensures that your agricultural operation receives supplies when you need them, minimizing downtime during critical seasons.
